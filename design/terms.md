# Terms — who is who

Decided 2026-10-24 (owner), in the owner's words:

- **author** — "the founder": Tankun Sriket.
- **writer** — "the developer, say that guy who made an app in CyborgPL": whoever writes a CyborgPL program.
- **contributors** — "the outside people contributing to CyborgPL".
- **authors** — "the entities that are the main contributors of CyborgPL (Tankun Sriket, design Claude, compiler
  Claude, etc)".
- **user** — "the user of the CyborgPL writer's final product": whoever runs a program a writer made.

So a message written for whoever builds the program is the writer's, and a program's output, its failed writes and its
exit codes are what its user sees. Design notes use these words in these senses from 2026-10-24; older text may say
"user" for the writer.
