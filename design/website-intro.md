> **We're just gonna be honest here.** Zag trades compile time for a great final product (though, no warranty). We're aiming for no hidden undefined behaviour, one of the fastest and safest final products through Zomp, and syntax that's easy to read. Not Python-like (hiding the machine for ease of use): a reader who knows the concepts can follow Zag without knowing Zag.
>
> Zomp is Zag's compiler, and it proves your program safe before it ships. Every array index, every division and every number that could overflow is proven safe, or the build fails. There's no flag to turn it off. If Zomp can't prove it, you handle it in the code, or it doesn't compile.
