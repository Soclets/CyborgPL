# Where the name CyborgPL comes from

The owner, 2026-10-02: "It's named CyborgPL, because it's easy to fit the
theme, the work force is human + Ai, and it has multiple ways to do a thing".

Three reasons, then:

1. **The theme is easy to fit.** Limb (a package), the org Cyborg Assembly &
   Manufacturing Site.
2. **The workforce is human + AI.** Half person, half machine.
3. **There is more than one way to do a thing.** A language bolted together
   from parts: `&` and `lend`, Rust's `i32` beside `int32`, four prints
   (`print.stored`, `print.useful`, `print.weird`, `print.format`), three
   check levels.

PL is Programming Language (CPL for short). Asked after a talk about where
other names come from (LuaJIT, Java, Julia, Kotlin, and Lilith, whose origin
won "most diabolical"); CyborgPL's was judged not diabolical at all. Not a
secret either: the owner said "add it in secrets anyways".
