CyborgPL is a hobby programming language: very slow at the development, fast and safe at the final product. Its compiler, CCA, runs Precog, a whole-program analysis that proves every overflow, division by zero and out-of-range read impossible, or refuses the program; the final build carries no runtime checks. It has ownership and borrowing, exact decimal numbers, SIMD, and a direct C interface, and compiles through LLVM.
